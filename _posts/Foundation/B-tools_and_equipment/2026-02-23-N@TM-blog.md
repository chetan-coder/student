## Blog post for N@TM

I only stayed at Night at the Museum for about 15 minutes, but I still noticed a lot. There were more people there than I thought there would be. It was loud and busy, but in a good way. Everyone was walking around looking at the different tables and trying out the games.

Each group made their own game, and they were all different. Some were more competitive and some were just fun and creative. I liked that they let people actually play the games instead of just talking about them. A few groups even showed parts of their code and explained how it worked, which was cool to see.

Since it was at night, it felt more like a real event and not just a school day. In the future, I think it would be cool if there were levels, high scores, or maybe even multiplayer games. Overall, even though I wasn’t there long, I could tell people worked hard and learned a lot.
